Poster Generator Engine — Final Skills

The final tool is a browser-based concert poster generator with editable typography, custom font import, image placement, manual drag/rotate/resize controls, concert-based randomization, multiple layout systems, SVG export, and PDF export.

Core Capabilities

  • 540 × 810 vertical poster canvas
  • Editable artist, headline, badge, venue, date, time, and city text
  • Built-in and imported TTF/OTF/WOFF/WOFF2 fonts
  • Manual text dragging, rotation, and resizing
  • Curved and tilted top/bottom typography
  • Custom colors and image insertion
  • Concert-based randomized content
  • Multiple randomized layout systems
  • SVG and PDF output
