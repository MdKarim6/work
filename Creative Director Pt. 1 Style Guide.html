BRANDSpotify (unofficial concept)
CAMPAIGNLifeTrack
LINEYour Life Has a Soundtrack.

Spotify LifeTrack is a fictional new listening mode that creates a personalized soundtrack for everyday life. By responding to factors such as time of day, local weather, movement, location context, and a mood chosen by the listener, LifeTrack continuously adjusts the music to fit the moment. The NYC-centered campaign turns familiar experiences—rainy subway rides, fast-paced commutes, nighttime walks, and rooftop sunsets—into cinematic scenes scored by Spotify. The visual identity combines atmospheric street photography, electric soundwave forms, and vivid color to explore the connection between music, emotion, and place.

05 / LOGOMARK

THE LIVING SOUNDWAVE.

PRIMARY SYMBOL + REVERSE TREATMENT
LifeTrack.PRIMARY / ON DARK
LifeTrack.REVERSE / ON GREEN

Concept: Six changing waveform bars represent music adjusting to everyday situations; the curved baseline suggests movement and continuity. The circle makes the symbol recognizable as an app icon, while Spotify green connects this fictional sub-brand to the parent brand. Keep the symbol simple and legible at small sizes. This is an original, unofficial campaign mark—not Spotify's official logo.
