01 / Artist Statement

The idea behind the patch

My patch represents Baruch College’s involvement in space exploration. I wanted to create a design that shows where our journey begins. The main focus of my patch is the Baruch Vertical Campus building, which represents our college, its students, and the community. I originally started with a simple New York City skyline but decided to use the actual Baruch building to make the design more meaningful and recognizable. The rocket launching from behind the building symbolizes ambition, exploration, and taking our knowledge beyond the classroom to outer space. I also included the Bearcat paw to represent school pride, teamwork, and Baruch's identity. I chose a square patch with a dark navy background to give it a clean, structured look while creating the feeling of outer space. Overall, the design shows the ideas of Baruch students leaving its campus to reach beyond earth.

02 / AI Disclosure

My creative process

Yes, AI tools were used during the development of my design. I began by drawing my own sketches, brainstorming symbols, and refining my initial concept by hand. I then used AI to help build an interactive mission patch generator using HTML & CSS. Through the generator, I experimented with different building designs, colors, fonts, layouts, and badge shapes.
